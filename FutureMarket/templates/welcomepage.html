About Future Marketing

Where Craftsmanship Meets Institutional Trust

Future Marketing is a premium textile manufacturer and the authorized distributor of Kitex products, headquartered in Meeyannoor, Kollam, Kerala.

For over a decade, we have specialized in high-volume, premium uniform manufacturing — partnering with schools, colleges, and healthcare institutions to deliver garments that combine durability, comfort, and precise institutional standards. Our in-house tailoring unit handles everything from single-school pilot runs to multi-campus rollouts of thousands of uniforms, all produced under strict quality control.

As an official Kitex distributor, we bring the same commitment to excellence to premium innerwear and casual wear, giving retail partners and institutions reliable access to one of South India's most trusted apparel brands.
